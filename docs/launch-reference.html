04 / DELIVERY MODEL

Evidence, not just scanner output.

01

Scope

Define owners, assets, permitted methods, exclusions, data boundaries and stop conditions.

02

Assess

Review configuration, source code and known risks within approved scope; use isolated validation environments.

03

Validate

Analyst reviews evidence, severity and business impact. Unverified results remain hypotheses.

04

Improve

Assign remediation owners, agree target dates, retest and communicate residual risk.

06 / GOVERNANCE

Support audit readiness, without false assurances.

Essential Eight

ASD's baseline mitigation guidance and maturity model inform scoped assessments. Evidence-based uplift is distinct from certification.

Privacy & NDB

Eligible data breaches may require notification for entities covered by the Privacy Act. ZERO supports readiness and evidence; legal decisions remain with the customer and advisers.

APRA CPS 234

For APRA-regulated entities, information security capabilities and systematic control testing are required. Tailor scope to applicable obligations.

NIST AI RMF

Voluntary risk management guidance for trustworthy AI systems and governance.

NIST SSDF

Secure software development practices support prevention, remediation and learning.

Confidentiality by design

Private evidence stores, scoped access, retention agreements and human review before external disclosure are required for live delivery.

  2. Agree commercial terms, assessment boundaries and data handling.
  3. Assign technical and business owners.
